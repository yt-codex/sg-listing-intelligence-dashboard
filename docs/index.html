How to read district signals

Pressure is the composite listing-side stress score for a district: fixed-reference price-cut and listing-persistence components; duplicate advertising and concentration are separate diagnostics.

Deterioration means the district pressure score has risen versus the prior weekly snapshot — a worse listing-side signal, not a transaction-price forecast.

Easing means the district pressure score has fallen versus the prior weekly snapshot. Treat it as possible clearing or lower seller stress, not proof that the market has recovered.

How to read project signals

Shortlist: up to 75 largest valid project/postal groups per market and segment. This is not an exhaustive development search; rankings are within this sample.

Pressure is the project/postal listing-side stress score: fixed-reference price-cut and listing-persistence components. Advertisement counts are not unit counts.

Deterioration means a project/postal group’s pressure score rose week-on-week, so it may deserve inspection.

Easing means the score fell week-on-week. It is a lead for possible clearing, not a confirmed improvement in transacted prices.

Low confidence rows have inadequate coverage, comparison validity, sample sizes or project matching; use them as triage leads only.
